On 15 November 2026, Room 120 of the Department of Banking and Insurance hosted a milestone for the department: the official launch of the BankSure Scientific Research Forum (BSRF).

Students filled the room for the first session. Faculty members spoke about why the department needs a research forum, introduced the forum’s chief advisor and laid out what members can expect in its first year.

## What happened

- **Opening talks.** Faculty members introduced the forum, its advisors and its four aims: research, knowledge sharing, collaboration and networking, and innovation for a better future.
- **Planning for higher study.** A session walked students through the GRE and what graduate programmes abroad look for.
- **The first badges.** The faculty panel handed BSRF badges to the forum’s first members.
- **Cake.** The day ended with everyone around a cake decorated with the forum’s logo.

## What comes next

The forum will announce its first research sessions and workshops on its [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61595603985680). Students and faculty of the department who want to take part can message the page to join.
